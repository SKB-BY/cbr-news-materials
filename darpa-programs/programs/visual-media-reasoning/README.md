## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> DARPA’s Visual Media Reasoning (VMR) program seeks to do just that by providing a software system that lets users ask queries of photo content, such as “What make and model of vehicle is that?” or “Is this person on our terrorist watch list?” or “Where is this building located?” If successful, VMR technology will serve as a force multiplier by extracting relevant information for human analysts and alerting them to scenes that warrant an analyst’s expert attention.
