## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Mechanisms for Amplification of fusion Reaction Rates in Solids (MARRS) program aims to explore new theoretical predictions and new data suggesting high fusion reaction rates may be possible in relatively low-temperature solids (less than ~2,000 degrees Kelvin).
