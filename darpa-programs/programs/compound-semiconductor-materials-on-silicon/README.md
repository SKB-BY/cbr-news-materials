## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The objective of the Compound Semiconductor Materials on Silicon (COSMOS) program is to develop a viable process for the fine-scale heterogeneous integration of compound semiconductor (CS) devices within standard Si complementary metal oxide semiconductor (CMOS) technology and to establish that this integration enables superior performance in specific mixed-signal circuit demonstrators.
