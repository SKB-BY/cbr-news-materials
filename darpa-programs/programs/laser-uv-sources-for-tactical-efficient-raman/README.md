## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The program will build on earlier successes in UV-generating materials and will explore novel concepts to develop a high beam-quality compact laser capable of achieving power greater than 1 watt, wall-plug efficiency greater than 10%, linewidth less than 0.01 nanometer (nm) and operating wavelengths between 220-240 nm.
