## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The APAQuS effort will harness the tunability and wide dynamic range of tabletop turbulence simulators, so called “quantum wind tunnels,” using ultracold quantum fluids or other quantum systems to generate experimental data that will guide data-informed models to better understand turbulence in various regimes.
