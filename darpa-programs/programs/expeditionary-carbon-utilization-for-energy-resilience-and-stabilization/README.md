## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> DARPA’s Expeditionary Carbon Utilization for Energy Resilience and Stabilization (ExCURSion) program seeks to combine the advantages of batteries and fuels by developing a portable, closed, rechargeable expeditionary power system which stores energy as carbon-based fuel.
