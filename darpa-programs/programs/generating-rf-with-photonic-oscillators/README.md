## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Generating RF with Photonic Oscillators for Low Noise (GRYPHON) program seeks to develop compact microwave frequency oscillators with extremely low phase noise to enable advanced sensing and communication applications.

Источник: [https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/generating-rf-with-photonic-oscillators](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/generating-rf-with-photonic-oscillators)
