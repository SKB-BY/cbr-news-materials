## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The goal of the Switch program is to develop a run-time reprogrammable biomanufacturing platform that affords flexible biosynthesis processes, which can toggle between ingest of various organic feedstocks or production of various chemicals, thereby enabling robust, rapidly repurposable manufacturing.
