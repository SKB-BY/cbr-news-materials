> The DARPA TFF program will address these challenges by seeking to develop a new composite material format (i.e., feedstock) and associated processing technologies (e.g., reconfigurable forming) to reduce manufacturing complexity and enable use of advanced materials for small parts weighing less than 20 pounds at costs competitive with aluminum.
