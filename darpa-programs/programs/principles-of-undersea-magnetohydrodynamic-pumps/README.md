## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The DARPA Principles of Undersea Magnetohydrodynamic Pumps (PUMP) program is developing electrode material system solutions capable of handling the extreme environment of operating a MHD drive in seawater under high electrical currents and magnetic fields.
