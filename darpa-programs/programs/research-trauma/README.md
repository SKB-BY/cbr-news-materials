## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Research Infrastructure for Trauma with Medical Observations (RITMO) program aims to combine large-volume multimodal sensor, intervention, and medical outcome data obtained from trauma patients during the early post-injury period into a single, de-identified database.

Источник: [https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/research-trauma](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/research-trauma)
