## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Knowledge Management at Scale and Speed (KMASS) program aims to research, develop, integrate, evaluate, and demonstrate underlying technology that will enable effective use of documented knowledge, acquisition of new knowledge as part of regular workflows, and application of useful knowledge when and where it is required and with necessary granularity.
