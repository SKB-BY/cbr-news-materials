## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The DARPA Multiobjective Engineering and Testing of ALloy Structures (Metals) program will develop foundational technologies to include material as an explicit, continuous variable in structural design optimization by leveraging recent advances in material testing and materials informatics.
