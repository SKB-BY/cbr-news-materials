## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The BioLogical Undersea Energy (BLUE) program seeks to develop power supplies that capture and convert microscopic forms of marine biomass (e.g., dissolved organic matter, phytoplankton, zooplankton) and other substances (e.g., microplastics, nutrients) into electrical power.
