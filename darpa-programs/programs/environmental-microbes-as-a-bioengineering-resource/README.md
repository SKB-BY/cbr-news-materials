## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Environmental Microbes as a BioEngineering Resource (EMBER) program will develop a biotechnology-based separation and purification strategy for Rare Earth Elements (REEs) from under-utilized domestic sources such as phosphate mine waste, acid mine drainage, and electronics recycling processes.
