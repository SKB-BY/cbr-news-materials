## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The AMPHORA program seeks to develop a portable, cold chain free system that can preserve any microbe (bacteria, fungus, virus) from any sample, environment, or clinical setting, increasing the DOW’s awareness of emerging threats and protect force health and readiness.
