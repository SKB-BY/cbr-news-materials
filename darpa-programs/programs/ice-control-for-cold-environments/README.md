## Назначение (цитата DARPA)

> The Ice Control for cold Environments (ICE) program seeks to develop solutions for extreme cold-weather DOW operational challenges by discovering and optimizing biologically sourced or inspired molecules to enable tuned inhibition or nucleation of ice crystallization, propagation, and adhesion.
