> The ultimate goal of the Platform for Acquisition, Logging, and Analysis of Devices for Inertial Navigation and Timing (PALADIN&T) initiative is the development of a universal testing platform to allow rapid, uniform early-stage Test and Evaluation (T&E) of prototypes for Positioning, Navigation, and Timing (PNT) tasks.
